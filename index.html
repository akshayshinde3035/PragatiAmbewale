About Pragati Foods

Who We Are

Pragati Foods was founded in 2013 by Mr. Swapnil Shinde with the vision of delivering fresh, hygienic, and high-quality frozen food products to customers across the region. Over the years, we have built a strong reputation for consistency, quality, and reliable supply.

Founders Vision

Our goal is to provide customers in and around our region with fresh and premium frozen food products while ensuring complete satisfaction through quality, hygiene, and ethical business practices.

Our Capabilities

We operate a modern processing unit equipped with automated pulp extraction, hygienic filling lines and a temperature-controlled cold storage facility. Our cold chain logistics and quality checks at every stage allow us to offer consistent product quality to hotels, retailers, and institutional buyers.

Manufacturing Facility

  • Location: Awasari Khurd, Pune, Maharashtra
  • Cold Storage Capacity: 120-ton facility maintained at –18°C

Our facility is equipped with modern infrastructure and temperature-controlled storage to maintain freshness, taste, and nutritional value.

Manufacturing & Quality

Raw Material Sourcing

We procure fresh mangoes directly from farmers in the Ratnagiri and Raigad regions, ensuring superior raw material quality from the start.

Processing Method

The mangoes are transported to our facility, where pulp extraction and packaging are carried out under controlled conditions. The pulp is blast-frozen at –40°C and then stored at –18°C, ensuring freshness, safety, and long shelf life.
